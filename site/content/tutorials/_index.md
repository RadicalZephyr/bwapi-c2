A [tutorial] takes you from nothing to something working, along one path, with every step shown
and every step known to work. It teaches by having you do; it does not explain alternatives, it
does not cover options you did not take, and it does not stop to justify its choices. When you
finish one you have a running bot and enough of a feel for the ABI to read the rest of the site.

What does not belong here: anything that starts from a task you already have (that is a how-to
guide), anything that lists facts for lookup (reference), and anything that argues a design
(explanation).

The first tutorials arrive with the first exports: a first bot in plain C, and a first bot in
Python over the raw bindings.

[tutorial]: https://diataxis.fr/tutorials/
