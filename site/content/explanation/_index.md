An explanation page is about understanding, not doing. It takes one design decision, says what
was decided, gives the reasons in the voice of a decision already made, and stops. Each page ends
with a link to the section of the plan or the research round it condenses, where the decision is
argued at length, with the numbers and the alternatives that lost.

What does not belong here: steps to follow (tutorials and how-to guides), and facts about any one
function (reference).
